# 📊 Financial Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Financial Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze and visualize financial performance across multiple business dimensions such as
revenue, region, gender, products, countries, and store rankings.

This dashboard helps users understand revenue trends, compare performance, and derive valuable business insights through dynamic visualizations and filters.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total revenue and average revenue.
* Compare revenue performance across regions and countries.
* Evaluate revenue contribution by gender.
* Analyze product-wise revenue performance.
* Examine store ranking based on revenue generation.
* Provide interactive business insights using visual analytics.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* **Power BI**
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
* **Data Modeling**
* **Data Visualization**
* **Excel / CSV Dataset**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

✔ Total Revenue
✔ Average Revenue
✔ Total Countries
✔ Total Transactions

### Visualizations

✔ Revenue by Region
✔ Revenue by Gender
✔ Revenue by Product & Rank
✔ Revenue by Country
✔ Revenue by Store Ranking

### Interactive Filters

✔ Year Filter
✔ Quarter Filter
✔ Store Rank Filter

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Asia** generated the highest revenue contribution among all regions.
* **Male customers** contributed a larger share of total revenue.
* **Store 1** achieved the highest revenue performance.
* Product-level analysis helps identify top-performing categories.
* Interactive filtering enables dynamic analysis by year, quarter, and store rank.
